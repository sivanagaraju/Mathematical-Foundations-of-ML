# Mathematical & Computational Glossary — Tutorial 13 : Pytorch - Training the Model

A structured, 6-column terminology reference connecting formal optimization theory, PyTorch runtime execution semantics, spoken English phonetic pronunciations, engineering analogies, and cross-references to dedicated mathematical dossiers in [`MathsTerms/`](../../MathsTerms/).

---

| Term / Syntax | Formal Mathematical Definition | Plain-English Software Meaning | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Real-World Engineering Analogy | Dedicated MathsTerm Link |
|:--------------|:-------------------------------|:-------------------------------|:---------------------------|:-------------------------------|:-------------------------|
| `Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM)` | The optimization principle $\theta^* = \operatorname{argmin}_\theta \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \ell(f_\theta(x_i), y_i)$ minimizing sample loss as a proxy for true distribution risk. | Training a model to minimize its average error across all available training examples. | *em-PEER-ih-kul RISK MIN-ih-my-zay-shun* | Adjusting an archer's sight based on the average spread of 100 practice shots on a shooting range. | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
| `Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)` | Iterative update $\theta_{t+1} = \theta_t - \eta g_t$ where $g_t = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}} \nabla \ell_i$ is an unbiased subgradient estimate. | An optimization algorithm that nudges weights in the direction that reduces error on a small random batch. | *stoh-KAS-tik GRAY-dee-unt dee-SENT* | Hiking down a foggy mountain by taking a step in the downhill direction indicated by a pocket compass. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| `Adam Optimizer` | Adaptive moment optimization combining exponentially decaying first moment $m_t$ and second moment $v_t$ with bias correction. | A smart optimizer that speeds up training by maintaining momentum and giving every weight its own custom step size. | *AD-um OP-tih-my-zer* | A heavy bowling ball rolling downhill with friction adaptation on varied terrain surfaces. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| `RMSProp Optimizer` | Optimization algorithm scaling parameter updates inversely by the square root of historical squared gradient moving average $v_t$. | An optimizer that prevents exploding updates by shrinking step sizes along dimensions with large historical gradients. | *AR-EM-ES PROP OP-tih-my-zer* | An automatic brake system that clamps down harder when descending dangerously steep hills. | [Exponential Moving Average (EMA)](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/10-Exponential_Moving_Average_EMA.md) |
| `Learning Rate (η)` | Positive scalar multiplier $\eta \in \mathbb{R}^+$ controlling parameter step magnitude along the descent direction. | The hyperparameter determining how large of a step the optimizer takes during each weight update. | *LEARN-ing RATE (AY-tuh)* | The gas pedal in a car: pressing it lightly moves cautiously, flooring it risks overshooting the turn. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| `Epoch` | Exactly one complete single pass over the entire training dataset $\mathcal{D}$ of cardinality $N$. | One complete sweep through every single training example in the dataset. | *EE-pok / EH-puk* | Reading through an entire textbook from the first page to the final index. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| `Iteration / Step` | A single parameter update computed over one mini-batch $\mathcal{B}$ of size $B$, requiring forward and backward passes. | One single mini-batch forward pass and weight update step within an epoch. | *IT-er-AY-shun / STEP* | Reading a single paragraph in a textbook and answering the practice question at the bottom. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| `optimizer.zero_grad()` | Mutator resetting `.grad` attributes of all tracked parameters to zero (or deallocating buffers with `set_to_none=True`). | Erases old gradients so the next backward pass doesn't accidentally add new gradients on top of old ones. | *OP-tih-my-zer DOT ZEER-oh GRAD* | Wiping a chalkboard completely clean before starting the next mathematical derivation. | [Derivatives, Gradients & Jacobians](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/02-Derivatives_Gradients_and_Jacobians.md) |
| `loss.backward()` | Reverse topological traversal on the dynamic computational DAG evaluating VJPs and accumulating into parameter `.grad` fields. | Computes how much every weight in the network contributed to the final loss error. | *LOSS DOT BACK-werd* | Retracing the path of a spilled liquid across a floor to find the exact pipe that leaked. | [Chain Rule & Backpropagation](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md) |
| `optimizer.step()` | In-place parameter mutation applying the optimizer-specific update equation $\theta_{t+1} \leftarrow \theta_t - \eta \Delta \theta$. | Takes the computed gradients and actually nudges all model weights to improve performance. | *OP-tih-my-zer DOT STEP* | Turning all the tuning pegs on a guitar by calculated increments to bring the strings into tune. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| `model.train()` | State flag setting `training=True` across all submodules, activating dropout sampling and batchnorm moment updates. | Tells PyTorch that the model is actively training, enabling randomized regularization techniques. | *MOD-yool DOT TRAYN* | Flipping the flight simulator into storm mode to practice flying in turbulent wind conditions. | [Functions & Rules](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/01-Functions_Derivatives_and_Rules.md) |
| `model.eval()` | State flag setting `training=False` across all submodules, disabling dropout and locking batchnorm running statistics. | Tells PyTorch that the model is being evaluated, turning off random noise and ensuring deterministic outputs. | *MOD-yool DOT EE-val* | Locking down the aircraft autopilot for a smooth, deterministic passenger flight. | [Functions & Rules](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/01-Functions_Derivatives_and_Rules.md) |
| `torch.no_grad()` | Thread-local context manager disabling dynamic DAG construction and activation caching during forward passes. | A memory-saving wrapper that turns off gradient tracking during evaluation and inference loops. | *TORCH DOT NO GRAD* | Taking an exam in practice mode where your answers are checked but no official permanent record is filed. | [Derivatives, Gradients & Jacobians](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/02-Derivatives_Gradients_and_Jacobians.md) |
| `state_dict` | Python `OrderedDict` mapping parameter and persistent buffer name strings to raw tensor data buffers. | A dictionary containing all the learned weights of a model, without any Python class code. | *STATE DIK-shun-air-ee* | A blueprint spreadsheet listing the exact dimensions and materials of every part in an engine. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
