# Glossary of Core Terms: Lecture 51 (Positional Embeddings)

A rigorous mathematical glossary defining the terminology, spoken phonetics, and operational definitions of Positional Embeddings, Permutation Equivariance, Frequency Encodings, and Cross-Attention Mechanics.

---

## Terminology Dictionary

| Term | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Mathematical Symbol | Formal Mathematical Definition | Course / Conceptual Context | Foundation Link |
|:-----|:---------------------------|:--------------------|:-------------------------------|:----------------------------|:----------------|
| **Positional Embedding** | *poh-ZISH-un-ul em-BED-ing* | $PE_{pos} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ | $f(pos): \mathbb{N}_0 \to \mathbb{R}^D$ mapping discrete sequence index to continuous coordinate vector. | Injects sequence ordinality and spatial coordinates into unordered Transformer token embeddings. | [09-Positional_Encodings.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/09-Positional_Encodings.md) |
| **Permutation Equivariance** | *per-myoo-TAY-shun ee-kwih-VAIR-ee-uns* | $f(\Pi X) = \Pi f(X)$ | Mathematical property where reordering inputs by permutation $\Pi$ produces the identical reordering of outputs. | Fundamental property of unpositioned self-attention that makes it blind to sequence token ordering. | [01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| **Sinusoidal Encoding** | *sigh-nyoo-SOY-dul en-KOH-ding* | $\sin(\omega_i t), \cos(\omega_i t)$ | Deterministic harmonic basis functions sampling geometric frequencies $\omega_i = 10000^{-2i/D}$. | Canonical non-trainable coordinate system introduced in Vaswani et al. supporting unbounded lengths. | [09-Positional_Encodings.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/09-Positional_Encodings.md) |
| **Wavelength Progression** | *WAYV-length proh-GRESH-un* | $\lambda_i = 2\pi / \omega_i$ | Geometric series $\lambda_i = 2\pi \cdot 10000^{2i/D}$ spanning wavelengths from $2\pi$ to $20000\pi$. | Multi-scale representation ensuring every sequence position receives a unique, collision-free coordinate. | [09-Positional_Encodings.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/09-Positional_Encodings.md) |
| **Linear Relative Shift Property** | *LIN-ee-er REL-uh-tiv SHIFT* | $PE_{pos+k} = M(k) PE_{pos}$ | Existence of an orthogonal rotation operator $M(k) \in \mathrm{SO}(2)$ transforming $PE_{pos}$ to $PE_{pos+k}$. | Allows linear self-attention layers to model relative token distances via standard matrix projections. | [01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| **Vector Superposition** | *VEK-ter soo-per-poh-ZISH-un* | $\tilde{x} = x + PE$ | Combining semantic word embeddings and positional coordinate vectors via element-wise addition. | Preserves tensor dimension $D$ without doubling projection parameters, relying on high-dimensional almost-orthogonality. | [08-Encodings_Categorical_and_Embeddings.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/08-Encodings_Categorical_and_Embeddings.md) |
| **Learned Positional Embeddings** | *LERN-d poh-ZISH-un-ul em-BED-ing* | $E_{\text{pos}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\max} \times D}$ | Trainable parameter matrix optimized by gradient descent via backpropagation (used in BERT and GPT-2). | Flexible data-driven coordinates that fail to extrapolate to sequences longer than training window $T_{\max}$. | [08-Encodings_Categorical_and_Embeddings.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/08-Encodings_Categorical_and_Embeddings.md) |
| **Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE)** | *ROH-ter-ee poh-ZISH-un em-BED-ing* | $R_{\Theta, m}^d q_m$ | Orthogonal block-diagonal rotation applied to query and key vectors enforcing $\langle R_m q, R_n k \rangle = g(q, k, m-n)$. | Modern position standard in foundation LLMs (LLaMA, Mistral) providing robust relative length extrapolation. | [01b-Basis_Spans_and_Orthogonality.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01b-Basis_Spans_and_Orthogonality.md) |
| **Self-Attention** | *SELF Uh-TEN-shun* | $\operatorname{Attn}(X W^Q, X W^K, X W^V)$ | Intra-sequence attention where queries, keys, and values are all linearly projected from the same sequence. | Core mechanism of Transformer encoders and decoder-only generative language models. | [03-Dot_Product_and_Similarity.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/03-Dot_Product_and_Similarity.md) |
| **Cross-Attention** | *KRAWS Uh-TEN-shun* | $\operatorname{Attn}(X_{\text{dec}} W^Q, X_{\text{enc}} W^K, X_{\text{enc}} W^V)$ | Inter-sequence routing where queries originate from one network and keys/values originate from another. | Crucial alignment bridge in encoder-decoder translation, image captioning, and latent diffusion models. | [03-Dot_Product_and_Similarity.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/03-Dot_Product_and_Similarity.md) |
