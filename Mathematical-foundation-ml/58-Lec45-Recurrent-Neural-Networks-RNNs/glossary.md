## 6-Column Mathematical Dictionary

| Term | Formal Definition | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Intuition | Code / Computational Representation | MathsTerms Cross-Link |
|:-----|:------------------|:---------------------------|:----------|:------------------------------------|:----------------------|
| **Recurrent Neural Network** | A neural network architecture featuring cyclical feedback connections that model sequential temporal dependencies via hidden state recurrence $h_t = \sigma(W_{hh} h_{t-1} + W_{xh} x_t + b_h)$. | "ree-KUR-ent NOO-rul NET-werk" | A brain that carries forward a running summary of past words while reading new words. | `nn.RNN(input_size=d, hidden_size=m)` | [`04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md`](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md) |
| **Latent Hidden State** | A latent memory vector $h_t \in \mathbb{R}^m$ acting as a lossy, fixed-dimensional sufficient statistic summarizing historical observations $(x_1, \dots, x_t)$. | "LAY-tent HID-un STAYT" | The mental scratchpad holding contextual understanding after processing the sequence up to step $t$. | `h_t = torch.tanh(W_hh @ h_prev + W_xh @ x_t)` | [`04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| **Temporal Parameter Sharing** | The architectural constraint forcing weight matrices $W_{hh}, W_{xh}, W_{hy}$ to remain identical across all time steps $t \in \{1, \dots, T\}$. | "TEM-poh-rul puh-RAM-uh-ter SHAIR-ing" | Applying the exact same grammar rule or audio filter regardless of when a word or sound appears. | `for t in range(T): h = cell(x[t], h)` | [`01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| **Unrolled Computational Graph** | The equivalent feedforward Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) formed by explicitly duplicating the recurrent cell across all $T$ temporal sequence steps. | "un-ROHLD kom-pyoo-TAY-shun-ul GRAF" | Unfolding a circular track into a long straight line of dominoes so you can trace fallbacks step by step. | `torch.autograd.backward(loss)` | [`04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md`](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md) |
| **Sequence Classification** | A supervised task mapping an entire sequence $X \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times d}$ to a single discrete categorical label $y \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ evaluated at terminal state $h_T$. | "SEE-kwens klas-ih-fih-KAY-shun" | Reading an entire book or movie review and giving it a single thumbs-up or thumbs-down rating. | `logits = fc(h[:, -1, :])` | [`06-Softmax.md`](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/06-Softmax.md) |
| **Seq2Seq Encoder-Decoder** | An architecture pairing an encoder RNN that compresses an input sequence into context $c = h_T$ with a decoder RNN that unpacks $c$ into an output sequence. | "SEEK-too-SEEK en-KOH-der dee-KOH-der" | A bilingual translator who listens to an entire sentence in English, grasps the concept, and writes it in French. | `Decoder(Encoder(x))` | [`04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| **End-Of-Sequence Token** | A reserved delimiter symbol `<EOS>` added to vocabulary $\mathcal{V}$ signaling the dynamic conclusion of variable-length sequence generation. | "END uv SEE-kwens dee-LIM-ih-ter" | The period at the end of a sentence that informs the speaker to stop talking. | `if token == EOS_IDX: break` | [`07-One_Hot_Encoding.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/07-One_Hot_Encoding.md) |
| **Auto-regressive Generation** | A sequence generation regime that models joint distribution $P(y_{1:T} \mid X)$ as a product of step-wise conditionals, feeding previous predictions as inputs. | "AW-toh ree-GRES-iv jen-er-AY-shun" | Writing a story one word at a time, where each new word depends on what you have written so far. | `x_next = sample(model(x_prev))` | [`03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md`](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md) |
| **Causal Block-Toeplitz Matrix** | A block lower-triangular matrix with identical subdiagonal blocks representing the linear transformation of an unrolled RNN. | "KAW-zul BLOK TOY-plits MAY-triks" | A staircase matrix that prevents future events from rewriting the past while keeping the physics constant. | `scipy.linalg.block_diag / toeplitz` | [`01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| **Information Bottleneck** | The informational loss occurring when a long variable-length sequence is forced into a fixed-capacity vector $c \in \mathbb{R}^m$. | "in-fer-MAY-shun BOT-ul-nek" | Trying to summarize a 500-page novel onto the back of a single business card, losing all fine details. | `context = encoder_outputs[-1]` | [`01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md`](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md) |
