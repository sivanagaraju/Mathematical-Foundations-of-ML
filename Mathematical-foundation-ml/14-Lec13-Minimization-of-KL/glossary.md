| **Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)** | $\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_\theta \sum_{i=1}^N \log p_\theta(x_i)$, maximizing sample log-likelihood. | Standard parameter estimation matching model parameters to observed dataset samples. | **MAK-sih-mum LY-klee-hood es-tih-MAY-shun** | Tuning radio antennas until the broadcast static reaches the absolute clearest volume. | [MLE](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/05-MLE.md) |
| **Law of the Unconscious Statistician (LOTUS)** | $\mathbb{E}_{X \sim p}[g(X)] = \int g(x)p(x)dx$ evaluating expectations of functions. | Computing batch averages `torch.mean(g(x))` without analytically working out the distribution of $g(X)$. | **LAW uv thee un-KON-shus stat-ih-STISH-un** | Calculating total grocery cart cost by multiplying prices by quantities without calculating overall receipt distributions. | [Random Variables & Distributions](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/01-Random_Variables_and_Distributions.md) |
| **Law of Large Numbers (LLN)** | $\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N g(X_i) \xrightarrow{\text{a.s.}} \mathbb{E}[g(X)]$ as sample size $N \to \infty$. | Monte Carlo batch empirical loss convergence: larger batches yield more accurate gradient estimates. | **LAW uv LARJ NUM-berz** | Flipping a coin 10,000 times; the proportion of heads inexorably settles at exactly 50%. | [Probability Basics & Axioms](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/00-Probability_Basics_and_Axioms.md) |
| **Data Entropy Invariance** | $\nabla_\theta H(p_{\text{data}}) = \mathbf{0}$, constant with respect to model parameters $\theta$. | Ground truth data entropy is fixed, so dropping it from the loss objective preserves the exact gradient. | **DAY-tuh EN-truh-pee in-VAIR-ee-uns** | Moving the zero-mark on a thermometer changes temperature numbers, but boiling heat remains identical. | [Entropy & Cross-Entropy](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md) |
| **Forward KL Divergence** | $D_{KL}(p_{\text{data}} \parallel p_\theta)$, relative entropy minimized in MLE. | Zero-avoiding objective forcing the model to stretch its probability mass to cover every observed data mode. | **FOR-werd KAY-EL** | An umbrella stretched wide enough to shield a large family standing in a scattered circle. | [KL Divergence](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/02-KL_Divergence.md) |
| **Reverse KL Divergence** | $D_{KL}(p_\theta \parallel p_{\text{data}})$, relative entropy minimized in VAE inference. | Zero-forcing objective driving the model to fit tightly into a single dominant peak of the target. | **ree-VERS KAY-EL** | A cautious hiker choosing one solid boulder rather than straddling two separate peaks. | [KL Divergence](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/02-KL_Divergence.md) |
| **Log-Likelihood** | $\ell(\theta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \log p_\theta(x_i)$, sum of log-densities. | Logarithm converting small floating-point probability products into numerically stable additions. | **LOG LY-klee-hood** | Using decibels to measure sound loudness rather than tiny micro-Pascal air pressure fluctuations. | [Likelihood & Log-Likelihood](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/04-Likelihood_and_Log_Likelihood.md) |
| **Empirical Distribution** | $\hat{p}(x) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \delta(x - x_i)$ placing probability mass $1/N$ on samples. | Discrete representation of raw training data enabling Monte Carlo expectation evaluation. | **em-PEER-ih-kul dis-trih-BYOO-shun** | A histogram built from customer survey responses. | [Random Variables & Distributions](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/01-Random_Variables_and_Distributions.md) |
| **Argmin Operator** | $\arg\min_\theta f(\theta)$ returning parameters minimizing the loss function. | PyTorch training optimization result finding parameters `theta` where loss achieves its floor. | **ARG-min OP-er-ay-ter** | The coordinates of the lowest depression in a valley. | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| **Surrogate Loss** | $\mathcal{L}_{\text{surr}}(\theta) = -\frac{1}{N}\sum \log p_\theta(x_i)$, computable objective. | Negative Log-Likelihood (NLL) serving as a proxy for the intractable true continuous KL divergence. | **SER-uh-gut LOSS** | A flight simulator allowing pilots to practice emergencies without crashing a real plane. | [NLL](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/06-NLL.md) |
