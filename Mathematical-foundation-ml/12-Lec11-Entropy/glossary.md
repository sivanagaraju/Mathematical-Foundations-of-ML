| **Surprisal (Self-Information)** | $I(A) = -\log_2 P(A)$, inversely related to event probability. | The number of bits required to encode an event; rare events deliver higher payload. | **suh-PRY-zul** | Hearing that a coin landed heads vs hearing that snow fell in the Sahara Desert. | [Entropy & Cross-Entropy](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md) |
| **Shannon Entropy** | $H(X) = -\sum_{i=1}^K p(x_i) \log_2 p(x_i)$, expected surprisal. | The average uncertainty or theoretical minimum average bits per token needed for lossless compression. | **SHAN-un EN-truh-pee** | The degree of unpredictability when rolling an unfair vs fair 6-sided die. | [Entropy & Cross-Entropy](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md) |

| **Bit (Shannon)** | $\log_2(1/p)$ fundamental unit of information base 2. | A single binary decision unit (0 or 1) in computing. | **BIT** | A single toggle switch that can either be strictly ON or OFF. | [Entropy & Cross-Entropy](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md) |
