## 6-Column Mathematical Dictionary

|:-----|:------------------|:---------------------------|:----------|:------------------------------------|:----------------------|
| **Multi-Channel Tensor** | A higher-order array $\mathcal{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{P \times Q \times R}$ representing a $P \times Q$ grid with $R$ physical or spectral channels. | "MUL-tee CHAN-ul TEN-sor" | A stack of color or medical slice grids sharing the same spatial coordinate canvas. | `torch.randn(B, C_in, H, W)` | [`04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| **Filter Bank** | An ordered collection of $L$ distinct convolutional kernels $\mathcal{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times k \times k \times R}$ optimized via ERM. | "FIL-ter BANK" | A toolkit of multiple spatial pattern detectors looking for different visual primitives. | `nn.Conv2d(in_c, out_c, k)` | [`01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md`](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md) |
| **Channel Marginalization** | The summation $\sum_{c=1}^R \langle X_{:,:,c}, K_{:,:,c} \rangle_F$ collapsing all input channels to a single scalar per spatial coordinate. | "CHAN-ul mar-jin-ul-eye-ZAY-shun" | Blending all color layers into one unified feature response number. | `torch.sum(patch * kernel, dim=1)` | [`02-Vector_Norms_and_Inner_Products.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/02-Vector_Norms_and_Inner_Products.md) |
| **Bayer CFA** | Physical optical filter mosaic alternating red, green, and blue micro-filters across a 2D CMOS sensor grid. | "BAY-er SEE EFF AY" | The tiny colored glass tiles placed over a digital camera sensor to capture RGB light. | `demosaic(raw_sensor_grid)` | [`04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md`](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| **Average Pooling** | Spatial downsampling operator $\mathcal{P}_{\text{avg}}(X) = \frac{1}{k_p^2} \sum X_{\text{patch}}$, equivalent to a fixed uniform convolution. | "AV-er-ij POOL-ing" | Replacing a block of neighboring pixels with their average color to blur out noise. | `nn.AvgPool2d(kernel_size=2)` | [`01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md`](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md) |
| **Max Pooling** | Non-linear downsampling operator $y = \max(X_{\text{patch}})$ routing upstream subgradients exclusively to the argmax position. | "MAKS POOL-ing" | Keeping only the most prominent or intense feature response in a local neighborhood. | `nn.MaxPool2d(kernel_size=2)` | [`07-Argmax.md`](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/07-Argmax.md) |
| **Residual Skip Connection** | Identity shortcut mapping $y = \mathcal{F}(x) + x$ creating an additive gradient highway preventing vanishing gradients. | "reh-ZID-yoo-ul SKIP kuh-NEK-shun" | An express bypass track allowing training signals to flow through deep networks unimpeded. | `x + self.block(x)` | [`04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md`](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md) |
| **Transposed Convolution** | The mathematical transpose $W^T x$ of a convolution matrix, computing fractionally strided spatial upsampling. | "trans-POHZD kon-vuh-LOO-shun" | Spreading out small feature activations into a larger spatial grid for image generation. | `nn.ConvTranspose2d(in_c, out_c, k)` | [`01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md`](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md) |
| **Fully Convolutional Network** | A neural network architecture devoid of dense fully connected layers, preserving spatial coordinate grids end-to-end. | "FULL-ee kon-vuh-LOO-shun-ul" | A deep vision model that produces 2D maps instead of flattening into single classification numbers. | `nn.Sequential(Conv, ReLU, Conv)` | [`01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md`](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md) |
| **U-Net Architecture** | An encoder-decoder architecture with a contracting path, bottleneck, and expanding decoder linked by lateral skip connections. | "YOO-net AR-kuh-tek-chur" | An hourglass network that compresses images to understand meaning, then expands to paint pixel masks. | `UNet(in_channels=3, classes=K)` | [`01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md`](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/01-Convolution_and_Pooling.md) |
