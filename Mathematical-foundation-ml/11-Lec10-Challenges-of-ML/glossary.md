| **Model Family** | $\mathcal{M} = \{p_\theta \mid \theta \in \Theta\}$, parameterized hypothesis class of candidate distributions. | A parametric neural architecture whose behavior is tuned by weight tensor `theta`. | **MOD-ul FAM-lih** | A customizable blueprint for a house where dimensions and rooms can be resized. | [Likelihood & Log-Likelihood](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/04-Likelihood_and_Log_Likelihood.md) |

| **Population Risk** | $R(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{X \sim p}[\ell(X; \theta)]$, expected loss over unseen true distribution. | The true real-world expected error rate of a deployed model across all future customer inputs. | **pop-yuh-LAY-shun RISK** | Driving performance in unknown terrain under all future weather conditions. | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
| **Generalization Gap** | $\Delta_{\text{gen}} = \lvert \hat{R}_N(\theta) - R(\theta) \rvert$, difference between test and train errors. | The degradation in accuracy when moving from familiar training data to fresh production traffic. | **jen-er-uh-ly-ZAY-shun GAP** | Scoring 100% on practice exams but only 75% on the real proctored exam. | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
