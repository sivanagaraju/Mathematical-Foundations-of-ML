## Mathematical Notation & Tensor Shape Legend

- $B$: Mini-batch size (number of independent sequences).
- $T$: Temporal sequence length (number of time steps).
- $D$: Input token/feature embedding dimension.
- $H$: Recurrent hidden state dimension.
- $L$: Number of stacked vertical recurrent layers (`num_layers`).
- $K$: Number of target prediction classes.
- $D_{\text{dir}}$: Direction factor ($1$ for unidirectional, $2$ for bidirectional).
- $\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times T \times D}$: Input mini-batch tensor under `batch_first=True`.
- $\text{output} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times T \times (D_{\text{dir}} \cdot H)}$: Sequence output from top layer across all time steps.
- $\mathbf{h}_n \in \mathbb{R}^{(L \cdot D_{\text{dir}}) \times B \times H}$: Terminal recurrent state across all vertical layers.
