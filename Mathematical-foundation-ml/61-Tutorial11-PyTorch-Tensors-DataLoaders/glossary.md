# Glossary of Terms & Mathematical Syntax — Tutorial 11 : Pytorch - Tensors and Data Loaders

A structured, 6-column terminology reference connecting formal linear algebra definitions, PyTorch runtime execution semantics, spoken English phonetic pronunciations, engineering analogies, and cross-references to dedicated mathematical dossiers in [`MathsTerms/`](../../MathsTerms/).

---

| Term / Syntax | Formal Mathematical Definition | Plain-English Software Meaning | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Real-World Engineering Analogy | Dedicated MathsTerm Link |
|:--------------|:-------------------------------|:-------------------------------|:---------------------------|:-------------------------------|:-------------------------|
| `torch.Tensor` | A multidimensional array $\mathcal{T} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0 \times d_1 \times \dots \times d_{k-1}}$ over a numerical field equipped with shape, stride, and device metadata. | The core PyTorch data structure wrapping contiguous memory with autograd tracking and GPU execution. | *TORCH TEN-ser* | A multi-dimensional spreadsheet that can be instantly beamed to a GPU accelerator. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `stride` | An integer tuple $s \in \mathbb{N}^k$ where $s_r = \prod_{j=r+1}^{k-1} d_j$, defining memory address jumps: $\Delta(i) = \sum i_r s_r$. | The number of elements in the flat 1D memory buffer skipped to advance by one unit along a specific dimension. | *STRYDE TUP-ul* | Pacing out footsteps across a tile floor: taking 1 step to move left/right, 4 steps to jump to the next row. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `@` (`torch.matmul`) | Bilinear inner-product contraction $C_{ij} = \sum_{p=1}^k A_{ip} B_{pj}$ mapping $\mathbb{R}^{m \times k} \times \mathbb{R}^{k \times n} \to \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$. | Matrix multiplication combining features across dimensions into linear combinations. | *AT OP-er-ay-tor / MAT-mul* | A commercial paint mixer: combining multiple color pigment streams into unified final hues. | [Vectors & Matrices](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| `*` (`torch.mul`) | Coordinate-wise Hadamard product $(A \odot B)_{ij} = A_{ij} B_{ij}$ for identical or broadcast-compatible shapes. | Element-by-element multiplication scaling each coordinate independently without cross-talk. | *AST-er-isk / HAD-uh-mard MUL-tih-ply* | An audio equalizer slider bank: raising or lowering individual frequency volumes without mixing them. | [Dot Product & Similarity](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/03-Dot_Product_and_Similarity.md) |
| `torch.from_numpy` | A bijective view mapping $T: \text{ndarray} \to \text{Tensor}$ sharing underlying storage pointer $P_0$ without allocating memory. | Creates a PyTorch tensor directly on top of an existing NumPy array's memory buffer in $O(1)$ time. | *TORCH FROM NUM-py* | Looking at the exact same physical house through two different windows (a NumPy window and a PyTorch window). | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `torch.cat` | Tensor concatenation operator $\mathcal{T} = [A, B]$ along axis $d \in \{0, \dots, k-1\}$, summing dimension sizes along $d$. | Glues multiple tensors together along an existing dimension (e.g., stacking rows or gluing feature columns). | *TORCH DOT KAT* | Splicing train cars together into a longer single train (dim=0) or widening cargo containers (dim=1). | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `dtype` | Numerical representation standard $\tau \in \{\text{float32}, \text{int64}, \dots\}$ specifying byte width and mantissa/exponent encoding. | Specifies whether numbers are stored as 32-bit floats, 64-bit integers, etc., determining memory footprint. | *DEE-type / DAY-tuh TYPE* | Selecting container size: using small 4-byte jars for standard liquids vs large 8-byte jars for double precision. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `device` | Hardware execution context $D \in \{\text{'cpu'}, \text{'cuda:0'}, \text{'mps'}\}$ defining physical memory placement. | Specifies which physical processor (CPU system RAM or GPU video RAM) holds and executes the tensor. | *TORCH DEE-vice* | The physical desk where work happens: your local home office desk (CPU) vs an industrial factory workbench (GPU). | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `Dataset` | Abstract base class representing an indexed map from sample index $i \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ to observation tuple $(x_i, y_i)$. | An object-oriented contract requiring `__len__` and `__getitem__` to load and transform data on demand. | *DAY-tuh-set KLASS* | A library card catalog: tells you how many books exist and fetches exactly book #14 when requested. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `DataLoader` | Stochastic batch sampling and collation operator $\pi: \mathcal{D} \to \{(X_b, Y_b)\}_{b=1}^{\lceil N/B \rceil}$. | A multi-process streaming engine that batches, shuffles, and prefetches samples for training loops. | *DAY-tuh LOH-der* | A sushi conveyor belt: kitchen workers prepare plates in the back and deliver small groups of dishes steadily to diners. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `transforms.ToTensor()` | Normalization mapping $T: [0, 255]^{H \times W \times C} \to [0.0, 1.0]^{C \times H \times W}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{C \times H \times W}$. | Converts raw integer pixel arrays to channel-first floating-point tensors scaled between 0.0 and 1.0. | *TRANS-forms TOO TEN-ser* | A currency exchange counter: converting foreign physical coins into digital standard bank units. | [Tensors & Shapes](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| `.item()` | Projection operator $\pi_{\mathbb{R}}: \mathcal{T} \in \mathbb{R}^{1} \to r \in \mathbb{R}$ extracting a single scalar primitive. | Extracts a 1-element tensor into a native Python `float` or `int`, breaking autograd graph retention. | *DOT EYE-tem* | Taking a single gold nugget out of a massive locked vault and putting it directly into your pocket. | [Vectors & Matrices](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
