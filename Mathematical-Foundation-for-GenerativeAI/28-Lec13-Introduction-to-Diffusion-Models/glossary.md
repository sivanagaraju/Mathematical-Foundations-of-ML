# Mathematical Glossary: Introduction to Diffusion Models

| Term | Pronunciation / Spoken Form | Mathematical Meaning | Intuition & Visual Metaphor | Common Traps / Misunderstandings | Related Concepts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diffusion Process** | *dih-FYOO-zhun PRAH-sess* | A continuous or discrete-time stochastic process that transforms an arbitrary distribution into Gaussian noise | Dropping food dye into water until it disperses uniformly | Confusing physical diffusion with numerical blur | [02-Markov_Chains_and_Stationary_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md) |
| **Perturbation Kernel** | *per-ter-BAY-shun KUR-nul* | The conditional distribution $q(x_t \mid x_{t-1})$ specifying the transition law | Adding a calibrated microscopic kick of Gaussian haze | Thinking the perturbation kernel has trainable weights | [02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Variance Schedule** | *VAIR-ee-unss SKED-yool* | The sequence of scalars $\beta_1, \dots, \beta_T$ controlling noise injection rate | The valve controlling how fast ink diffuses into liquid | Believing constant $\beta$ is optimal | [01-Lipschitz_Continuity.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Primal-Analysis-and-Foundations/06-Lipschitz_Continuity.md) |
| **Jump Kernel** | *JUMP KUR-nul* | The closed-form marginal distribution $q(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0, (1-\bar{\alpha}_t)I)$ | Taking a time machine directly to year $t$ without walking every day | Forgetting that noise is compounded, not added linearly | [02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Reverse Process** | *ree-VURS PRAH-sess* | The generative Markov chain $p_\theta(x_{t-1} \mid x_t)$ learned by a neural network | Playing a film of an ink drop dissolving in reverse | Assuming reverse transitions are non-Gaussian | [01-Latent_Variable_Models_and_ELBO.md](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/05-Latent_Variable_Models.md) |
| **Markov Chain** | *MAR-koff CHAYN* | A sequence of stochastic events where $P(X_{t} \mid X_{1:t-1}) = P(X_t \mid X_{t-1})$ | A traveler with amnesia who only remembers their previous city | Assuming diffusion relies on hidden RNN memory | [02-Markov_Chains_and_Stationary_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md) |
| **Isotropic Gaussian** | *eye-so-TROP-ik GOW-see-un* | A multivariate Gaussian whose covariance is proportional to identity $\sigma^2 I$ | A perfectly spherical cloud of dust with no preferred direction | Assuming non-diagonal covariance in standard DDPM | [02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Score Function** | *SKOR FUNK-shun* | The gradient of the log probability density with respect to data $\nabla_x \log p(x)$ | A compass vector pointing uphill toward higher data probability | Confusing score function with scalar reward or loss | [01-Lipschitz_Continuity.md](../../MathsTerms/01-Primal-Analysis-and-Foundations/06-Lipschitz_Continuity.md) |
