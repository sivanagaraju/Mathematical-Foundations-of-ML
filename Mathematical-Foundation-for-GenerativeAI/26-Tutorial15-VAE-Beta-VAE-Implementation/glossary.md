# Glossary: Mathematical Terminology for Tutorial 15 VAE & Beta-VAE Implementation

This glossary defines formal mathematical terms, phonetic pronunciations, software engineering analogies, theoretical invariants, and dedicated concept links for Tutorial 15.

| Term | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Formal Definition | Plain-English Software Meaning | Mathematical Guarantee / Invariant | Dedicated MathsTerm Link |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Reparameterization Trick | **ree-PAR-am-et-er-ih-ZAY-shun TRIK** | $z = g_\phi(x, \epsilon) = \mu_\phi(x) + \sigma_\phi(x) \odot \epsilon, \quad \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$ | Isolating the random dice roll into an external variable so autograd can take derivatives of the mean and variance | Differentiable parameterization: $\nabla_\phi \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi}[f(z)] = \mathbb{E}_{p(\epsilon)}[\nabla_\phi f(g_\phi(x, \epsilon))]$ | [Reparameterization Trick](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/08-Reparameterization_Trick.md) |
| Log-Variance Parameterization | **LOG VAIR-ee-uns par-am-et-er-ih-ZAY-shun** | Network outputs $\log \sigma^2 \in \mathbb{R}^K$; computes $\sigma = \exp(0.5 \log \sigma^2)$ | A software encoding guaranteeing that standard deviation is always strictly positive without hitting negative numbers | Positive standard deviation: $\sigma > 0$ strictly for all real-valued network outputs | [Logarithms & Exponential Functions](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/00-Logarithms_and_Exponential_Functions.md) |
| Analytical Gaussian KL | **an-uh-LIT-ih-kul GAW-see-un KAY-EL** | $D_{KL} = -\frac{1}{2} \sum_{k=1}^K (1 + \log \sigma_k^2 - \mu_k^2 - \sigma_k^2)$ | The exact pencil-and-paper math formula measuring how far the encoder's bell curve is from the standard normal bell curve | Non-negative: $D_{KL} \ge 0$, with equality if and only if $\mu_k = 0$ and $\sigma_k^2 = 1$ for all $k$ | [KL Divergence](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/02-KL_Divergence.md) |
| Beta Hyperparameter | **BAY-tuh hy-per-puh-RAM-et-er** | Scalar multiplier $\beta > 0$ on the relative entropy term: $\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{recon} + \beta D_{KL}$ | A software dial controlling how much pressure is applied to squeeze and organize the latent representation | Standard VAE when $\beta = 1.0$; acts as a Lagrange multiplier in constrained optimization | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
| Factor Disentanglement | **FAK-ter dis-en-TANG-gul-ment** | Latent factorization $q(z|x) = \prod_k q(z_k|x)$ aligning with ground-truth factors | Dedicating single dials to single physical concepts: dial 1 for rotation, dial 2 for thickness, dial 3 for scale | Zero total correlation across latent coordinates: $\text{TC}(Z) \to 0$ | [Vectors & Matrices](../../MathsTerms/01-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| Latent Traversal | **LAY-tent truh-VER-sul** | Linearly sweeping $z_k \in [-3, 3]$ while holding all other latent dimensions $z_{j \ne k} = 0$ | Systematically turning one specific knob on the synthesizer from min to max to hear what sound attribute it controls | Continuous manifold inspection: evaluates generative smoothness and semantic isolation | [Autoencoders & Latent Spaces](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/03-Autoencoders_and_Latent_Spaces.md) |
| Bernoulli Reconstruction Loss | **ber-NOO-lee ree-kon-STRUK-shun LOSS** | $\mathcal{L}_{recon} = -\sum x \log \hat{x} + (1 - x) \log(1 - \hat{x})$ (Binary Cross-Entropy) | Treating each black-or-white pixel as the probability of a coin toss being heads | Suitable for normalized $[0, 1]$ images with high binary contrast | [Entropy & Cross-Entropy](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/01-Entropy_CrossEntropy_CCE.md) |
| Gaussian Reconstruction Loss | **GAW-see-un ree-kon-STRUK-shun LOSS** | $\mathcal{L}_{recon} = \frac{1}{2\sigma_x^2} \|x - \hat{x}\|_2^2$ (Mean Squared Error) | Measuring the squared physical distance between original pixel values and generated pixel values | Equivalent to negative log-likelihood under spherical Gaussian observation noise | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
