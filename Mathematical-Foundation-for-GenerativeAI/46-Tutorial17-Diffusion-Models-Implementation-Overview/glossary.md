# Glossary — Tutorial 17: Implementation Overview of Diffusion Models

| Term | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Mathematical Definition | Plain English Intuition | Context in Lecture | MathsTerms Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hierarchical VAE** | hye-er-AR-kih-kul V-A-E | $p_\theta(x_0) = \int p_\theta(x_{0:T}) dx_{1:T}$ | Multi-stage latent model where latents preserve identical dimensions to data. | Instructor contrasts standard bottleneck VAEs with diffusion latents. | [Latent Variable Models](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/05-Latent_Variable_Models.md) |
| **Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO)** | EH-vih-dens LOH-er bownd | $\mathbb{E}_{q}[\log \frac{p(x_{0:T})}{q(x_{1:T}\mid x_0)}] \le \log p(x_0)$ | Tractable lower bound on log-likelihood maximized during training. | Lecture derives the 3-term ELBO breakdown (reconstruction, prior, consistency). | [Latent Variable Models](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/05-Latent_Variable_Models.md) |
| **Consistency Term** | kun-SIS-tun-see turm | $\sum_t \mathbb{E}_{q(x_{t-1}, x_t | x_0)} [D_{KL}(q(x_t|x_{t-1}) \parallel p_\theta(x_t|x_{t+1}))]$ | Original transition discrepancy term plagued by dual-variable variance. | Instructor highlights why this term creates unacceptable Monte Carlo variance. | [Joint Marginal Conditional Dist](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md) |
| **Denoising Term** | dee-NOY-zing turm | $\sum_t \mathbb{E}_{q(x_t | x_0)} [D_{KL}(q(x_{t-1}|x_t, x_0) \parallel p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t))]$ | Variance-reduced ELBO term evaluating reverse steps conditioned on clean $x_0$. | Derived using Bayes' rule as Equation 58 in Calvin Luo's paper. | [Joint Marginal Conditional Dist](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md) |
| **Mean Estimation** | meen es-tih-MAY-shun | $\mathcal{L}_{\mu} = \|\mu_q(x_t, x_0) - \mu_\theta(x_t, t)\|^2$ | Parameterization where the neural network directly outputs the posterior mean. | Equation 93 of Calvin Luo reviewed in lecture. | [Common Probability Distributions](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Sample Estimation** | SAM-pul es-tih-MAY-shun | $\mathcal{L}_{x} \propto \|x_0 - \hat{x}_\theta(x_t, t)\|^2$ | Parameterization where the neural network predicts the original clean image $x_0$. | Equation 99 of Calvin Luo reviewed in lecture. | [Common Probability Distributions](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Noise Estimation** | noyz es-tih-MAY-shun | $\mathcal{L}_{\epsilon} = \|\epsilon_0 - \epsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\|^2$ | Parameterization where the neural network predicts the added standard Gaussian noise. | Standard DDPM objective (Eq 130) adopted in practice. | [Common Probability Distributions](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Score Estimation** | skor es-tih-MAY-shun | $s_\theta(x_t, t) \approx \nabla_{x_t} \log q(x_t)$ | Parameterization estimating the score vector field, equal to $-\epsilon_\theta / \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}$. | Equation 148 bridging score matching to DDPM. | [Common Probability Distributions](../../MathsTerms/04-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
