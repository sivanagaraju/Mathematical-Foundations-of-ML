# Mathematical Glossary: Diffusion Models Part 1 (ELBO & Posterior)

| Term | Pronunciation / Spoken Form | Mathematical Meaning | Intuition & Visual Metaphor | Common Traps / Misunderstandings | Related Concepts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Evidence Lower Bound** | *EV-ih-dens LOH-er BOWND* | $\mathbb{E}_q[\log(p/q)]$, a tractable concave lower bound on log-marginal likelihood $\log p(x_0)$ | A safety net that sits guaranteed beneath an unmeasurable ceiling | Believing ELBO can exceed true log-likelihood | [05-Latent_Variable_Models.md](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/05-Latent_Variable_Models.md) |
| **Telescoping Sum** | *TEL-eh-skoh-ping SUM* | A series where consecutive terms cancel, leaving only initial and final boundary values | Collapsing a handheld brass spyglass into its outer tube | Forgetting that boundary terms do not cancel | [03-Functions_Derivatives_and_Rules.md](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/01-Functions_Derivatives_and_Rules.md) |
| **Tractable Posterior** | *TRAK-tuh-bul pos-TEER-ee-er* | The distribution $q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0)$ conditioned on both $x_t$ and ground truth $x_0$ | Pinpointing a driver's halfway location when both start and finish cities are known | Believing $q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t)$ is tractable without knowing $x_0$ | [03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/03-Joint_Marginal_Conditional_Dist.md) |
| **Prior Matching Term** | *PRY-er MATCH-ing TURM* | $L_T = D_{\text{KL}}(q(x_T \mid x_0) \parallel p(x_T))$, measuring divergence between final noisy state and prior | Checking that soup is boiled completely until only steam remains | Attempting to backpropagate gradients into $L_T$ | [02-KL_Divergence.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/02-KL_Divergence.md) |
| **Denoising Term** | *dee-NOYZ-ing TURM* | $L_{t-1} = D_{\text{KL}}(q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) \parallel p_\theta(x_{t-1} \mid x_t))$, penalizing reverse step errors | A flight simulator grading how closely an autopilot follows a landing trajectory | Assuming $L_{t-1}$ requires numerical integration | [02-KL_Divergence.md](../../MathsTerms/04-Information-Theory-and-Divergences/02-KL_Divergence.md) |
| **Reconstruction Term** | *ree-kun-STRUK-shun TURM* | $L_0 = -\log p_\theta(x_0 \mid x_1)$, assessing final denoising step recovery of clean data | Removing the final smudge from a freshly cleaned glass pane | Thinking $L_0$ uses isotropic noise prior | [06-NLL.md](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/06-NLL.md) |
| **Posterior Mean** | *pos-TEER-ee-er MEEN* | $\tilde{\mu}_t(x_t, x_0)$, the analytical expected centroid of $x_{t-1}$ | The weighted midpoint on a tightrope stretched between $x_0$ and $x_t$ | Confusing posterior mean with model prediction $\mu_\theta$ | [02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
| **Posterior Variance** | *pos-TEER-ee-er VAIR-ee-unss* | $\tilde{\beta}_t = \frac{1 - \bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} \beta_t$, residual Gaussian spread around posterior mean | The thickness of a pencil line drawn between two known points | Assuming $\tilde{\beta}_t = \beta_t$ at all time steps | [02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md](../../MathsTerms/03-Probability-and-Statistical-Estimation/02-Common_Probability_Distributions.md) |
