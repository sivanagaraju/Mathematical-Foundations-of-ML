# Glossary: Mathematical Terminology for Tutorial 14 WGAN Gradient Penalty

This glossary defines formal mathematical terms, phonetic pronunciations, software engineering analogies, theoretical invariants, and dedicated concept links for Tutorial 14.

| Term | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Formal Definition | Plain-English Software Meaning | Mathematical Guarantee / Invariant | Dedicated MathsTerm Link |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Gradient Penalty | **GRAY-dee-ent PEN-ul-tee** | $\mathcal{L}_{GP} = \mathbb{E}_{\hat{x}}[(\|\nabla_{\hat{x}} D(\hat{x})\|_2 - 1)^2]$ | A soft mathematical leash penalizing the Critic whenever its slope strays away from 1.0 | Drives the Critic toward unit gradient norm $\|\nabla_{\hat{x}} D\|_2 = 1.0$ almost everywhere along transport paths | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |

| Unit Gradient Norm | **YOO-nit GRAY-dee-ent NORM** | $\|\nabla_x f(x)\|_2 = 1.0$ | The function slopes at exactly a 45-degree angle, changing at a rate of 1 unit per unit distance | Exact saturation of the 1-Lipschitz bound: $\|f\|_L = 1.0$ | [Derivatives Gradients & Jacobians](../../MathsTerms/02-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/02-Derivatives_Gradients_and_Jacobians.md) |
