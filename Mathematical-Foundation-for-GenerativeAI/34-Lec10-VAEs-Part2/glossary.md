# Glossary: Mathematical Terminology for Lec 10 VAEs Part 2

This glossary provides formal definitions, spoken pronunciation guides, software intuitions, mathematical guarantees, and cross-references for the load-bearing concepts in Lecture 10 Part 2.

| Term | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Formal Definition | Plain-English Software Meaning | Mathematical Guarantee / Invariant | Dedicated MathsTerm Link |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) | **EL-boh** | $\mathcal{L}(\theta, \phi; x) = \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi(z\|x)}[\log p_\theta(x\|z)] - D_{KL}(q_\phi(z\|x) \parallel p(z))$ | A differentiable objective function that balances reconstruction accuracy against latent space organization | $\mathcal{L}(\theta, \phi; x) \le \log p_\theta(x)$ with equality iff $q_\phi(z\|x) = p_\theta(z\|x)$ almost everywhere | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
| Reparameterization Trick | **ree-puh-RAM-ih-tuh-ry-ZAY-shun trik** | Coordinate transform $z = g_\phi(\epsilon, x) = \mu_\phi(x) + \sigma_\phi(x) \odot \epsilon$ where $\epsilon \sim p(\epsilon) = \mathcal{N}(0, I)$ | Moving the random noise generator outside the neural network so backpropagation gradients can flow through mean and variance | $\nabla_\phi \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi}[f(z)] = \mathbb{E}_{p(\epsilon)}[\nabla_\phi f(g_\phi(\epsilon, x))]$ via LOTUS | [Chain Rule and Backpropagation](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md) |
| Recognition Encoder | **rek-ug-NISH-un en-KOH-der** | Differentiable neural network mapping data vector $x \in \mathbb{R}^D$ to posterior distribution parameters $(\mu_\phi(x), \log \sigma_\phi^2(x)) \in \mathbb{R}^{2K}$ | A neural feature compressor that outputs distribution statistics rather than a single fixed code | Bounded variance outputs under suitable final activation layers | [Functions and Derivatives](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/01-Functions_Derivatives_and_Rules.md) |
| Generative Decoder | **JEN-er-uh-tiv dee-KOH-der** | Differentiable neural mapping from latent realization $z \in \mathbb{R}^K$ to observation parameters $\hat{x}_\theta(z) \in \mathbb{R}^D$ | A neural synthesizer that reconstructs high-dimensional raw data points from low-dimensional coordinates | Smooth continuous mapping from compact latent space to data manifold | [Tensors and Shapes](../../MathsTerms/02-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/04-Tensors_and_Shapes.md) |
| Kullback-Leibler Divergence | **KOOL-bak LY-bler dy-VER-jenss** | $D_{KL}(Q \parallel P) = \int q(z) \log \frac{q(z)}{p(z)} \, dz$ measuring relative entropy between two probability distributions | A penalty measuring how much probability mass $q$ wastes assuming distribution $p$ | $D_{KL}(Q \parallel P) \ge 0$ with equality iff $Q = P$ almost everywhere (Gibbs Inequality) | [Probability Basics and Axioms](../../MathsTerms/01-Primal-Analysis-and-Foundations/01-Probability_Basics_and_Axioms.md) |
| Log-Variance Parameterization | **LOG VAIR-ee-enss puh-RAM-ih-ter-eye-ZAY-shun** | Outputting unbounded scalar $s = \log \sigma^2 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that standard deviation is obtained as $\sigma = \exp(0.5 \cdot s)$ | Predicting variance in unconstrained log-space so exponential transform strictly enforces positivity | $\sigma > 0$ strictly guaranteed for all $s \in (-\infty, +\infty)$ | [Logarithms and Exponentials](../../MathsTerms/01-Primal-Analysis-and-Foundations/02-Logarithms_and_Exponential_Functions.md) |
| Posterior Collapse | **pos-TEER-ee-er kuh-LAPS** | Failure mode where $q_\phi(z\|x) \to p(z)$ for all $x$, causing mutual information $I(X; Z) \to 0$ and decoder to ignore $z$ | The encoder gives up compressing input and outputs default Gaussian noise, while decoder outputs blurry dataset average | $D_{KL}(q_\phi(z\|x) \parallel p(z)) \to 0$ across all training batches | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| Ancestral Sampling | **an-SES-truh-l SAM-pling** | Forward sampling chain: $z \sim p(z)$ followed by $x \sim p_\theta(x\|z)$ | Simulating a generative process from causes to effects without observing any initial ground truth data | Generates asymptotically exact samples from the model joint distribution $p_\theta(x, z)$ | [Probability Basics and Axioms](../../MathsTerms/01-Primal-Analysis-and-Foundations/01-Probability_Basics_and_Axioms.md) |
| Mean Squared Error (MSE) | **MEEN SKWARED AIR-er** | $\text{MSE}(x, \hat{x}) = \frac{1}{D} \sum_{d=1}^D (x_d - \hat{x}_d)^2$ | Euclidean distance penalty measuring geometric deviation between target and prediction | Equivalent to negative log-likelihood under independent Gaussian noise with variance $\sigma^2 = 1$ | [Vector Norms and Inner Products](../../MathsTerms/02-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/02-Vector_Norms_and_Inner_Products.md) |
| Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) | **BY-nuh-ree KROSS EN-truh-pee** | $\text{BCE}(x, \hat{x}) = -\sum_{d=1}^D [x_d \log \hat{x}_d + (1 - x_d) \log(1 - \hat{x}_d)]$ | Cross-entropy loss measuring binary pixel probabilities | Equivalent to negative log-likelihood under independent Bernoulli observations | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
