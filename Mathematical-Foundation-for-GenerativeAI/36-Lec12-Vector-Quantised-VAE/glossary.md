# Glossary: Mathematical Terminology for Lec 12 Vector Quantised VAE

This glossary defines formal mathematical terms, phonetic pronunciations, software engineering analogies, theoretical invariants, and dedicated concept links for Lecture 12.

| Term | Spoken English (Phonetics) | Formal Definition | Plain-English Software Meaning | Mathematical Guarantee / Invariant | Dedicated MathsTerm Link |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Vector Quantization | **VEK-ter kwon-tih-ZAY-shun** | $q(z) = rg\min_{e_k} \|z - e_k\|_2$ | Mapping a continuous feature vector to the nearest discrete prototype vector in a pre-defined codebook dictionary | Voronoi tessellation partitions $\mathbb{R}^D$ into $K$ deterministic non-overlapping polyhedral regions | [Encodings Categorical & Embeddings](../../MathsTerms/02-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/08-Encodings_Categorical_and_Embeddings.md) |
| Discrete Codebook | **dih-SKREET KOHD-buuk** | $\mathcal{E} = \{e_k\}_{k=1}^K \subset \mathbb{R}^D$ | A learnable matrix of $K$ distinct embedding vectors of dimension $D$ representing discrete categorical prototypes | Embedding weight matrix of fixed rank and finite discrete capacity $\log_2 K$ bits per spatial position | [Vectors & Matrices](../../MathsTerms/02-Linear-Algebra-Geometry-and-Tensors/01-Vectors_and_Matrices.md) |
| Straight-Through Estimator | **STRAYT THROO ESS-tih-may-ter** | $z_q = z_e + \text{sg}[z_q - z_e]$ | A gradient surrogate technique that copies upstream gradients directly to upstream inputs during backpropagation | $\nabla_{z_e} \mathcal{L} \equiv \nabla_{z_q} \mathcal{L}$ on backward pass despite zero derivative of argmin | [Chain Rule & Backpropagation](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/04-Chain_Rule_and_Backpropagation.md) |
| Commitment Loss | **kuh-MIT-ment LOSS** | $\beta \|z_e - \text{sg}[e]\|_2^2$ | A regularizer penalizing the encoder output for drifting away from the currently assigned codebook cluster center | Enforces bounded encoder growth: $\nabla_e \mathcal{L}_{commit} = 0$ strictly via stop-gradient $\text{sg}[e]$ | [Loss Functions](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/08-Loss_Functions.md) |
| VQ Codebook Loss | **VEE-KYOO KOHD-buuk LOSS** | $\|\text{sg}[z_e] - e\|_2^2$ | The L2 objective pulling the selected codebook vector towards the empirical continuous encoder representation | Updates dictionary vectors exclusively: $\nabla_{z_e} \mathcal{L}_{vq} = 0$ via stop-gradient $\text{sg}[z_e]$ | [Gradient Descent](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/09-Gradient_Descent.md) |
| Exponential Moving Average Update | **ek-spoh-NEN-shul MOO-ving AV-er-ij** | $e_k^{(t)} = \frac{m_k^{(t)}}{N_k^{(t)}}$ | An online tracking method updating codebook vectors via running sums and counts of assigned vectors | Replaces SGD on dictionary: updates converge to batch cluster centroids without optimizer learning rates | [Exponential Moving Average EMA](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/10-Exponential_Moving_Average_EMA.md) |
| Codebook Collapse | **KOHD-buuk kuh-LAPS** | $\exists k : p(z=k) = 0$ | The failure state where a large proportion of codebook vectors are never selected as nearest neighbors | Inactive centroids receive zero updates under standard SGD, permanently stranding model capacity | [Argmax](../../MathsTerms/03-Multivariate-Calculus-and-Optimization/07-Argmax.md) |
| Autoregressive Prior | **aw-toh-ree-GRES-iv PRY-er** | $p(z_1, \dots, z_M) = \prod_{m=1}^M p(z_m \mid z_{<m})$ | A generative sequence model (e.g. PixelCNN or Transformer) that models joint probability over discrete spatial indices | Strictly causal probability factorization satisfying probability axioms: $\sum_{\mathbf{z}} p(\mathbf{z}) = 1$ | [Autoregressive Models](../../MathsTerms/06-Deep-Architectures-and-Generative-Models/04-Autoregressive_Models.md) |
