## 🎯 The Honest Starting Point

To begin the entropy chapter, you need basic discrete probability and logarithms. Later chapters introduce additional requirements when they first become necessary: expectations and likelihood for KL, convexity and Fenchel conjugacy for $f$-divergence and VDM, and norms plus Lipschitz continuity for Wasserstein distance.

You do **not** need measure-theoretic differential geometry or abstract Borel probability metrics to begin. Every divergence, informational quantity, and transport cost is grounded with intuitive physical analogies, spoken pronunciations, pencil-and-paper micro-numerical examples with backward gradients, and executable code in both pure Python standard library and production PyTorch.

## 🤖 What These Concepts Unlock in Modern AI

- **Autoregressive Large Language Models (LLMs):** Cross-entropy next-token prediction and forward KL divergence minimization against empirical web text.
- **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) & RLHF:** Reverse KL divergence penalty constraints $\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi_\theta \parallel \pi_{\mathrm{ref}})$ preventing language models from drifting into reward-hacking gibberish.
- **Variational Autoencoders (VAEs):** Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) with analytical Gaussian KL divergence regularization $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mathcal{N}(\mu, \Sigma) \parallel \mathcal{N}(0, I))$.
- **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs, LSGAN, WGAN-GP):** Jensen-Shannon divergence in standard GANs, variational $f$-divergence estimation in $f$-GANs / LSGAN, and Kantorovich-Rubinstein dual optimal transport in Wasserstein GANs.
- **Diffusion Models & Flow Matching:** Reverse-time score matching as equivalent to minimizing Fisher divergence and relative entropy along the probability flow ODE.
