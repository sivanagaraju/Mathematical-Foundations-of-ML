Why Naive Distances Fail:

❌ 1. Subtraction: ∫(P - Q)dx = 0

Because both valid densities integrate to 1.0, positive and negative errors cancel out to exact zero, even for garbage outputs!

⚠️ 2. Absolute Difference: ∫|P - Q|dx

Discontinuous step gradients (+1 or -1) lack magnitude info; backpropagation oscillates and freezes on neural network weights.

✨ 3. Csiszár's Likelihood Ratio: u = P(x)/Q(x)

The relative scaling ratio is coordinate-invariant, detects missing modes (u ≫ 1), and punishes fake hallucinations (u ≪ 1).

💡 The 3 Axioms of the Penalty Bowl:
  • Zero Fine: $f(1.0) = 0$ (Match = zero penalty).
  • Convexity: $f''(u) \ge 0$ (Deviations rise).
  • Expectation: $D_f = \mathbb{E}_Q[f(u)] \ge 0$ (Jensen).
